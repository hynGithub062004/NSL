CAC LENH BAT GOI MINH CHUNG (CHAY TREN DUNG NODE; CTRL+C DE DUNG)

1. Chup R1 - eMBB (sau khi chon eMBB):
   tcpdump -ni eth1 -vv 'ip6 and ip6[6] == 43'
   Client: ping -c 5 2001:db8:40::2
   Doi: outer dst 2001:db8:200::2, SRH type 4, segleft=1.

2. Chup R2 - eMBB:
   tcpdump -ni eth1 -vv 'ip6 and ip6[6] == 43'
   Client: ping -c 5 2001:db8:40::2
   Doi: outer dst 2001:db8:400::4, segleft=0.

3. Chup R1 - URLLC (sau khi chon URLLC):
   tcpdump -ni eth2 -vv 'ip6 and ip6[6] == 43'
   Client: ping -c 5 2001:db8:40::2
   Doi: outer dst 2001:db8:300::3, segleft=1.

4. Chup R3 - URLLC:
   tcpdump -ni eth1 -vv 'ip6 and ip6[6] == 43'
   Client: ping -c 5 2001:db8:40::2
   Doi: outer dst 2001:db8:400::4, segleft=0.

5. Chup R4 - sau End.DX6 (ca hai policy):
   tcpdump -ni eth2 -vv 'icmp6 and (host 2001:db8:10::2 or host 2001:db8:40::2)'
   Client: ping -c 5 2001:db8:40::2
   Doi: IPv6 goc echo request Client -> Server va echo reply chieu nguoc.

Luu y:
- Chay lenh tcpdump truoc, sau do moi ping.
- Trong `tcpdump`, "packets dropped by kernel" la thong ke bat goi, khong dong nghia mat goi mang.
- Traceroute co the hien dau * do co che SRv6; xac minh bang ping + tcpdump.
- Huong ve tu Server -> Client mac dinh qua R4 -> R2 -> R1 (ca khi policy xuoi qua R3).
